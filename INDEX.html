Complete Navigation Index - All Working Routes & Features

⚡ Quick Start

  1. New User? Start with Sign Up or Sign In
  2. Looking for Events? Browse Events, Hotels, or Restaurants
  3. Already booked? View your Bookings & Tickets
  4. Event organizer? Go to Partner Dashboard to manage your events
  5. Check in guests? Use Ticket Check-in

🔐 Authentication

  • Sign In Login with email & password /auth/signin
  • Sign Up Create a new account /auth/signup
  • Forgot Password Reset your password /auth/forgot-password

👤 My Dashboard (Guest)

  • Dashboard Home Your bookings & overview /dashboard Authenticated
  • My Bookings All your bookings (events, hotels, restaurants) /dashboard/bookings Authenticated
  • Edit Booking Modify booking details or cancel /dashboard/bookings/[bookingId] Authenticated

🎫 Tickets & Check-in

  • Digital Event Ticket View QR code & check-in code for event /tickets/[bookingId] Authenticated
  • Check-in (Guest) Scan QR or enter code to check in /checkin/[token] Public

🏨 Browse & Book

  • Events Browse & book events /events
  • Hotels Browse & request hotel stays /hotels
  • Restaurants Browse & request restaurant reservations /restaurants

👨‍💼 Partner Dashboard

  • Partner Home Overview & shortcuts /partner Partner
  • Ticket Check-in Scan QR codes or enter codes to check guests in /partner/checkin Partner

🎪 Manage Events

  • Event Bookings View & manage event tickets & attendees /partner/events/[eventId]/bookings Partner

🏩 Manage Hotels

⭐ Key Features

🎫 Digital Tickets: QR codes with fallback 8-character alphanumeric codes for offline venues

✅ Check-in System: Real-time guest check-in with idempotent operations

💰 Payment Integration: M-Pesa sandbox + Flutterwave with webhook confirmations

📧 Email Confirmations: Automatic confirmations via Resend for all bookings

🏨 Multi-Service: Events, Hotels, Restaurants in one platform

👨‍💼 Partner Portal: Event organizers can manage bookings and check in guests

📊 Seed Data: 8 top Nairobi hotels + 10 top + 10 new restaurants + Kenyan events
